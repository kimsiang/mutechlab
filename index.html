03项目案例Deployments

在黄浦江底，探测器读出了潮汐Beneath the Huangpu, the detector read the tide

上海外环隧道是黄浦江下的沉管隧道。我们把便携式双层塑料闪烁体探测器带进隧道，在不影响通车的情况下扫描缪子通量。结合 Geant4 模拟，观测到缪子通量与潮位之间清晰的反相关：江水每涨一米，通量下降约 4%。The Shanghai Outer Ring Tunnel is an immersed tube beneath the Huangpu River. We carried a portable dual-layer plastic scintillator detector inside and scanned the muon flux while traffic kept flowing. Together with Geant4 simulation, the data show a clear anti-correlation between muon flux and tide: each metre of rising water cuts the flux by about 4%.

  1. N-01

    加速器缪子成像（SHINE）Accelerator muography at SHINE

    宇宙线成像慢，是因为每平方厘米每分钟只有约一个缪子。利用 SHINE 电子束打靶产生的 GeV 级缪子，模拟显示约 3×10⁵ 个事例、约 6 小时即可得到结构相似度 SSIM > 0.9 的图像。Cosmic-ray muography is slow: about one muon per cm² per minute. With GeV muons from SHINE's electron beam on target, simulation shows about 3×10⁵ events, roughly six hours, give an image with SSIM > 0.9.

  2. N-02

    闪烁光纤散射层析（SciFi-MST）Scintillating-fibre scattering tomography

    对钢筋混凝土做散射密度切片成像，看清内部钢筋与缺陷。Scattering-density slices of reinforced concrete that reveal rebar and defects inside.

  3. N-03

    缪子“世界模型”Muon world model

    用神经网络替代模型加速正演与反演，缩短从数据到图像的时间。Neural surrogates that speed up forward modelling and inversion, shortening the path from data to image.

09常见问题FAQ

客户最常问的问题What clients ask first

Q1能探多深？How deep can it see?

缪子能穿透数百米岩石，深度很少是限制，真正的限制是时间：越深，到达的缪子越少，测量越久。可行性速算可以给您一个量级。Muons pass through hundreds of metres of rock, so depth is rarely the limit. Time is: deeper targets receive fewer muons. The feasibility tool gives the order of magnitude.

Q2能看多细？How fine is the detail?

分辨率取决于探测器角分辨率、与目标的距离和测量时长。可行性报告会给出具体数值。It depends on angular resolution, distance and measurement time. The feasibility report gives concrete numbers.

Q3有辐射吗？Is there a radiation risk?

没有额外辐射。我们只接收天然宇宙射线缪子，设备不发射射线，也无需放射源许可。No. We only receive natural cosmic-ray muons; the equipment emits nothing and needs no source licence.

Q4会影响运营吗？Will it disrupt operations?

不会。设备装在检修通道或设备间，静默运行，只需供电和网络。No. Equipment sits in service corridors, runs silently and needs only power and network.
