天然缪子Cosmic-ray muons

一个更重、更短命的“胖电子”A heavier, short-lived cousin of the electron

宇宙射线撞击高层大气，产生大量缪子。它和电子带同样的电荷，质量却大得多。正因为重，它几乎不被物质散射，能一路深入地下。Cosmic rays strike the upper atmosphere and produce showers of muons. A muon carries the electron's charge but far more mass. Because it is heavy, it barely scatters and travels deep underground.

缪子成像原理How muography works

越厚越密，缪子越少Denser ground lets fewer muons through

原理与医院的 CT 相似。缪子穿过物质时会损失能量，低能缪子停在途中。数一数从各个方向到达探测器的缪子，就能反推出路径上的密度。The idea is close to a hospital CT scan. Muons lose energy in matter and the slower ones stop. Counting the muons that arrive from each direction reveals the density along each path.

  1. 1在地下或目标旁布置探测器，长时间记录缪子径迹。Place detectors underground or beside the target and record muon tracks over time.
  2. 2与空旷处的“开天”通量比较，得到各方向的透射率。Compare with the open-sky flux to get transmission in each direction.
  3. 3通过模拟与神经网络反演，重建三维密度分布。Invert with simulation and neural networks to rebuild a 3D density map.
