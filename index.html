为什么选缪子成像Why muography

别的方法看不到的深度，缪子可以It reaches depths other methods cannot

钻探只看一个点，雷达只看浅层，声呐要上船、要封航。缪子从天上来，穿透混凝土、水和土层，从隧道内部就能持续测量。Boreholes see one point, radar sees the shallow layer, and sonar needs a boat on the water. Muons come from the sky, pass through concrete, water and soil, and can be measured continuously from inside the tunnel.

案例 · 上海外环隧道Case study · Shanghai Outer Ring Tunnel

在黄浦江底，探测器读出了潮汐Beneath the Huangpu, the detector read the tide

我们在外环隧道越江段内布设缪子探测器。江水每涨一米，到达探测器的缪子就少一点。这说明系统足够灵敏，可以追踪隧道上方质量的变化。We installed muon detectors in the river-crossing section of the Outer Ring Tunnel. Each metre of rising tide removed a measurable share of muons. The system is sensitive enough to track changes in the mass above the tunnel.

常见问题FAQ

客户最常问的五个问题Five questions clients ask first

能探多深？How deep can it see?

缪子能穿透数百米岩石，深度本身很少是限制。真正的限制是时间：越深，到达的缪子越少，测量越久。上面的速算工具可以给您一个量级。Muons pass through hundreds of metres of rock, so depth is rarely the limit. Time is: deeper targets receive fewer muons and take longer. The feasibility tool above gives you the order of magnitude.

能看多细？How fine is the detail?

分辨率取决于探测器的角分辨率、与目标的距离和测量时长。通常距离越近、测得越久越清晰。我们在模拟评估阶段会给出具体数值。Resolution depends on the detector's angular resolution, distance to the target and measurement time. Closer and longer is sharper. We give concrete numbers in the simulation study.

有辐射吗？安全吗？Is there any radiation risk?

没有额外辐射。我们只“接收”天然存在的宇宙射线缪子，探测器本身不发射任何射线，也不需要放射源许可。No added radiation. We only receive natural cosmic-ray muons. The detectors emit nothing and need no radioactive-source licence.

会影响隧道运营吗？Will it disrupt operations?

不会。探测器安装在检修通道或设备空间，静默运行，只需供电和网络。安装通常可安排在夜间维护窗口。No. Detectors sit in service corridors or equipment rooms and run silently, needing only power and network. Installation fits a night maintenance window.
